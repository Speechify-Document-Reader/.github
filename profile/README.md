## Overview

Speechify turns scattered reading tasks into a clear listening space. With Speechify text to speech and Speechify document reader workflows in mind, readers can play books, open files, and scan pages with less guesswork than juggling many tools.

Most reading tools handle one format and ignore photos, scanned pages, or long PDFs. **Speechify** solves this fragmentation by reading many sources in one app.

If you are tired of format limits and silent scans, **Speechify** is for you.
